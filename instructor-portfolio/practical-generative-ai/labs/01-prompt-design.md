# Lab 1 — Prompt Design

## Goal

Turn an underspecified request into a well-bounded AI task.

## Starting Prompt

> Write an email about tomorrow's event.

## Step 1 — Identify what is missing

List at least four pieces of information the AI would otherwise need to assume.

## Step 2 — Rewrite the task

Use:

- Task
- Context
- Source
- Constraints
- Output
- Verification

## Step 3 — Compare

Explain why your revised request is safer or more useful than the original.

## Success Criteria

Your revised task should reduce unsupported assumptions without becoming unnecessarily complicated.
