# Lab 3 — From Prompt to Workflow

## Goal

Convert a useful AI prompt into a repeatable human-in-the-loop process.

## Scenario

Your team uses AI to draft a weekly external communication.

## Build the Workflow

Define:

1. Human goal
2. Required source material
3. AI task
4. Verification step
5. Risk or policy check
6. Human approval gate
7. External action
8. Recovery step if an error is found

## Diagram

```text
Human goal
   ↓
Source material
   ↓
AI draft
   ↓
Verification
   ↓
Risk / policy check
   ↓
Human approval
   ↓
External action
```

## Success Criteria

The workflow does not allow the AI to turn drafting permission into sending or publishing authority.
