# Performance Rubric

| Dimension | Beginning | Developing | Proficient | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task design | Vague request | Some context/constraints | Clear task, source, constraints, output | Adds appropriate verification and risk boundaries |
| Evaluation | Accepts output largely at face value | Notices obvious issues | Separates facts, assumptions, and unknowns | Prioritizes issues by consequence |
| Verification | Generic “double check” | Uses one check inconsistently | Matches checks to output type | Uses independent evidence/tests proportionate to risk |
| Responsible AI | Identifies a general risk | Names privacy or accuracy concerns | Identifies privacy, authorization, hallucination, or injection risks | Selects realistic controls and residual risks |
| Workflow design | One-shot prompt | Adds manual review | Defines verification and approval steps | Defines boundaries, recovery, and final human responsibility |

## Evidence Standard

A learner is **Proficient** when they can independently design a bounded AI task, identify unsupported assumptions, choose a suitable verification method, and place human approval appropriately.
