# Pre-Assessment

Answer briefly.

1. What makes you trust an AI-generated answer?
2. What is one task you currently use—or could use—AI for?
3. If an AI gives you a source citation, what should you do before relying on it?
4. What is the difference between asking AI to draft something and authorizing AI to send it?
5. Name one kind of information you would hesitate to place into an AI system.

This assessment is diagnostic. Its purpose is to help the instructor adjust the workshop.
