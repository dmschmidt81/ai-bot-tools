# Post-Assessment

## Part 1 — Explain

1. Why can a fluent AI response still be wrong?
2. Name two ways to reduce unsupported assumptions in an AI task.
3. Why should a correction be rechecked?

## Part 2 — Apply

You ask an AI assistant to summarize a policy document and recommend a change.

Identify:

- the source of truth,
- one likely failure mode,
- the verification method,
- whether human approval is required,
- and who remains responsible for the final decision.

## Part 3 — Transfer

Choose one real task from your work.

Write a short AI-assisted workflow using:

**Input → AI → Verification → Approval → Action**
